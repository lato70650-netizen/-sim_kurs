## Запрос генерации

Create a clean 2 by 2 photographic contact sheet, exactly four equally sized rectangular photographs edge to edge, no gutters no borders no text no logos. Photorealistic editorial photography, sunny vibrant teal orange yellow palette, contemporary landscaped urban park, natural people and anatomically realistic equipment. Each quadrant must work independently as a website photograph. TOP LEFT: wide editorial shot of two 12-13 year old teenagers standing beside their individual electric scooters, helmets securely fitted, relaxed cheerful discussion in a park, stationary not riding, bright orange and turquoise clothing, generous blue sky and leafy trees. TOP RIGHT: appealing close-up product photograph of a stationary electric kick scooter beside a single electric unicycle and an electric skateboard, on a paved park plaza, realistic distinct devices, no people. BOTTOM LEFT: close medium shot of one helmeted teenager wearing an orange jacket, standing beside a stationary electric kick scooter, checking the brake lever with adult supervision, daylight. BOTTOM RIGHT: wide shot of helmeted teenager standing beside electric scooter at a beautiful separated park path junction, contemplating route, bright yellow jacket, lush green surroundings. No road traffic, no stunt riding. High end genuine camera photographic look, rich saturated colors, realistic details, no illustrated or rendered aesthetic. All four quadrants equally sharp. Landscape overall image.
